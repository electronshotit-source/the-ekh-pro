The Photographer

Known to his clients simply as Archie — The Archigrapher is a fashion and portrait photographer who treats every set like a building under construction: foundations first, then form, then light.

Trained to notice how a doorway frames a body before he ever picked up a camera, Archie brings an architect's sense of structure to fashion editorial — clean lines, deliberate negative space, and a respect for the geometry that clothing creates on a moving body.

Based in Lagos and working across Nigeria and beyond, his lens has covered independent designer lookbooks, runway coverage, brand campaigns and personal portrait sittings — always in service of one idea: that fashion photography is architecture you can wear.

How We Work

From Brief to Final Frame

Five steps, every time — the same discipline whether the set is a single portrait or a twenty-look campaign.

01

Concept

A call to define the story, the market, and the mood — references gathered, references cut.

02

Mood Board

Light, location and wardrobe locked into one board before anyone books a call sheet.

03

Shoot Day

A run of show built for the light you actually have, not the light you hoped for.

04

Select & Retouch

A tight edit first, then colour and skin work that respects texture over perfection.

05

Delivery

Full-resolution files, web-ready exports, and a licence clear enough to use everywhere you need it.
