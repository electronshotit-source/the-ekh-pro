The engine

It looks at the pixels. Not the filename.

Most tools ask you to pick the photos. This one has an opinion — and shows you why.

Nine signals per frame

Laplacian variance for sharpness. A 64-bin histogram for exposure and clipping. Hasler–Süsstrunk for colour. Skin-tone ratio in YCbCr — which works across every skin tone, not just pale ones — plus real face detection where the browser offers it.

  • Soft and badly exposed frames are vetoed outright, whatever else they score
  • Burst duplicates collapse to their single best frame via perceptual hashing
  • Survivors are sequenced into a narrative, not sorted by score

Questions

The things people ask.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?
No. Every part of the analysis — scoring, face detection, duplicate matching, the Reel render — happens in your browser using Canvas and Web Audio. Your gallery never touches a server. That is also why it works on hotel wifi.
Is the music actually free to use?
Yes, because it isn't licensed music — the app synthesises it with the Web Audio API at the exact length your reel needs. There is no rights holder to clear and nothing for Instagram's Content ID to match. If you upload a commercial track instead, we tell you plainly that it may get muted, and give you a one-click “export silent” so you can add the trending sound in-app where the reach is anyway.
Will the writing sound like everyone else's?
That is the failure mode we designed against. Copy is built from culture packs with specific vocabulary, and shaped by facts derived from your actual gallery — how dark it was, how saturated, how many frames you culled. Set your voice rules and banned words, and it writes to those. You can also plug in your own Claude API key and have Opus 5 write it against the same fact sheet.
What if the engine picks the wrong photos?
You see every score and every reason — the five bars under each frame are sharpness, exposure, colour, subject and composition. Filter by shot type, look at what was cut and why, and rebuild. It is an opinionated first pass, not a lock.
Does it work for non-Nigerian weddings?
Yes. Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa/Fulani, Ghanaian and a general destination pack ship today, and the tone control is independent of the culture pack. The Nigerian packs are the deepest because that is the market we built it for first.
Do I need to install anything?
No. It is a web app with no build step and no dependencies. Open it and it runs. Weddings are saved in your browser's own storage, so refreshing costs you nothing — though that also means clearing site data will remove them.
